Guide

Tuberculosis

Latent TB testing and treatment, standard treatment with weight-banded doses, liver toxicity, and isolation, for adults.

Checked November 2026Main source: NICE NG33

A guide only. Manage TB with your local TB service; children and drug-resistant TB need specialist care.

Sources: NICE NG33 Tuberculosis (updated 2024); BNF; Rifater SmPC; Nahid P et al. ATS/CDC/IDSA treatment of drug-susceptible TB (Clin Infect Dis 2016); Saukkonen JJ et al. ATS hepatotoxicity of antituberculosis therapy (Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2006); Joint Tuberculosis Committee of the BTS (Thorax 1998).
