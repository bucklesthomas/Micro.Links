Notes on the names

  • Phylum names changed in 2021 to end in “-ota”: Firmicutes became Bacillota, Proteobacteria became Pseudomonadota, and Bacteroidetes became Bacteroidota.
  • Enterobacterales is the order that holds Enterobacteriaceae, Yersiniaceae (Serratia) and Morganellaceae (Proteus, Morganella, Providencia): the “CPE” and “ESBL-producing Enterobacterales” in infection control.
  • Clostridioides difficile was moved out of Clostridium (and its family) in 2016, which is why it sits in Peptostreptococcaceae.
  • Stenotrophomonas is listed under Lysobacteraceae, which now includes the former Xanthomonadaceae.
  • Mycoplasma pneumoniae has a proposed new name, Mycoplasmoides pneumoniae; most laboratories still report Mycoplasma.

Source: List of Prokaryotic names with Standing in Nomenclature (LPSN, lpsn.dsmz.de); Oren A, Garrity GM. Valid publication of the names of forty-two phyla of prokaryotes. Int J Syst Evol Microbiol 2021;71:005056. Organism details on the organisms page.
