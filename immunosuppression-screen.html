Infection screen before immunosuppression

Tick the planned treatment and a few patient factors to get the tests to send, what to do with the results before starting, vaccines and their timing, and prophylaxis during treatment.

Checked November 2026Main sources: Green Book, EULAR 2022, EASL 2017

Adults. A checklist to support, not replace, the prescribing team's protocol and specialist advice (hepatology, TB service, transplant team).

Sources: UKHSA Green Book chapters 6 (contraindications: live vaccines and immunosuppression), 7 (immunisation of individuals with underlying medical conditions), 25 (pneumococcal, October 2026: PCV20) and 28a (shingles: Shingrix for severely immunosuppressed adults from 18 years, from September 2025); EULAR 2022 recommendations for screening and prophylaxis of chronic and opportunistic infections in adults with autoimmune inflammatory rheumatic diseases (Fragoulis et al., supporting review RMD Open 2022); EASL 2017 clinical practice guidelines on hepatitis B; MHRA Drug Safety Update: rituximab, screen for hepatitis B before treatment; UKHSA migrant health guide (helminth infections); NICE NG33 (tuberculosis).
