Penicillin allergy

A teaching page to work through with medical students on the ward: PEN-FAST, the types of reaction, cross-reactivity, and de-labelling, with a case and a quiz. Includes a “going deeper” section and a key trial for final-year students.

Sources: Trubiano JA et al. Development and validation of a penicillin allergy clinical decision rule (PEN-FAST). JAMA Intern Med 2020;180:745–52; BSACI guideline for the management of suspected penicillin allergy (2022); NICE CG183 Drug allergy. Local antibiotic choices follow your trust policy.
