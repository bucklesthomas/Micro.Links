Teaching

Teach on the ward

A ready-made teaching session for medical students on the ward round. Pick a topic and how long you have: you get a timed plan, questions to ask with answers to reveal, key points, something to show at the bedside and a take-home message.

Checked November 2026Aimed at medical students; links to the full guides on this site

Content follows the guides on this site and UK sources including NICE (sepsis NG51, pneumonia NG250, UTI NG109 and NG111, cellulitis NG141, C. difficile NG199, meningitis NG240, TB NG33), UKHSA guidance and the UK malaria guidelines. Teach from the patient in front of you where you can, and check local policy for antibiotic choices.
