Asplenia and hyposplenism

Vaccines and their timing around splenectomy, antibiotic prophylaxis by age, standby antibiotics and advice for the patient, with a plan to copy into Epic.

Checked November 2026Main source: Green Book chapters 7 and 25

Who this applies to

  • Splenectomy for any reason, congenital asplenia, and functional hyposplenism: sickle cell disease and other haemoglobinopathies, coeliac disease with hyposplenism, and conditions that may lead to splenic dysfunction.
  • Main risks: Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae and Neisseria meningitidis; also Capnocytophaga canimorsus after dog bites, severe malaria and babesiosis.

Sources: UKHSA Green Book chapter 7, immunisation of individuals with underlying medical conditions (practical schedule for asplenia or splenic dysfunction); Green Book chapter 25, pneumococcal (October 2026: PCV20 for risk groups and every 5 years in asplenia); Public Health Agency Northern Ireland, splenectomy in adults and children: factsheet for health professionals (antibiotic prophylaxis and patient advice).
