A starting point for the differential, not a full risk list. Outbreaks change week to week: check TravelHealthPro outbreak news and the UKHSA VHF algorithm before ruling anything out.

Notes

  • Malaria risk is the UKHSA advice for travellers (chemoprophylaxis, or bite avoidance and awareness). Any fever after travel to a malaria area still needs malaria testing, even after low-risk travel or full prophylaxis.
  • Haemorrhagic fever flags show where Lassa, Crimean-Congo haemorrhagic fever or MERS occur, or where Ebola or Marburg outbreaks have happened. The VHF algorithm decides the actual risk.
  • MDR-TB flags follow the WHO list of high MDR/RR-TB burden countries. Carbapenemase flags mark countries with high reported prevalence; screen after any hospital stay abroad regardless.
